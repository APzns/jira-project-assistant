# Stakeholders

## VP Product — Executive Sponsor
- **Owns:** Conversion / revenue outcomes.
- **Cares most about:** Checkout Redesign (APS-1) landing on time.
- **Watch for:** Any slip to M1 or a stalling Checkout epic.

## Security & Compliance Lead
- **Owns:** The external compliance deadline.
- **Cares most about:** Security & Compliance epic (APS-2) and zero open
  High/Highest bugs.
- **Watch for:** R1 triggers — compliance behind, or open critical bugs.

## Engineering Manager
- **Owns:** Team capacity and delivery predictability.
- **Cares most about:** Stable velocity and a manageable overdue backlog.
- **Watch for:** R2 (velocity dip) and R3 (overdue backlog) triggers.

## Program Manager (you)
- **Owns:** Overall delivery, cross-workstream coordination, risk reporting.
- **Cares most about:** Balancing both epics against fixed deadlines.
