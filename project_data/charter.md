# Project Charter: Horizon


Project Horizon - Commerce Platform Modernization

Project Horizon is an R&D program to modernize our commerce platform end to end. It brings six capability areas — checkout redesign, security & compliance, performance hardening, a unified analytics platform, mobile parity, and onboarding improvements — onto a single modern foundation, delivered through a phased plan that runs from discovery and architecture through integration, hardening, and a controlled go-live. The program's north star is a reliable, secure, high-performance commerce experience that reaches parity across web and mobile, with a phased launch completing in Q4 2026.

Delivery is organized into four milestones. M0 – Preparation covers discovery, requirements, and architecture setup. M1 – Checkout Redesign delivers the flagship customer-facing rebuild. M2 – Security & Compliance hardens the platform and closes regulatory gaps. M3 – Launch-Ready completes core launch features, integration and migration, hardening and UAT, and go-live.

## Goal
Deliver a redesigned, secure checkout experience that increases conversion
and meets payment-security compliance, launch-ready by end of Q3 2026.

## Business Context
The current checkout has high cart abandonment and does not meet new
payment-security compliance standards. This program addresses both through
two parallel workstreams.

## Milestones

| Milestone | Target Date | Key Deliverables |
|-----------|------------|------------------|
| M0 – Preparation | 2026-07-17 | Discovery, requirements, architecture setup |
| M1 – Checkout Redesign | 2026-07-31 | Flagship customer-facing checkout rebuild (APS-1) |
| M2 – Security & Compliance | 2026-08-28 | Platform hardening, regulatory gap closure (APS-2) |
| M3 – Launch-Ready | 2026-10-09 | Integration, migration, hardening, UAT, go-live |

## Planned Delivery

- Sprints 1–2: Front-load Checkout Redesign (APS-1) capacity; light Security & Compliance ramp-up (per Decision D1).
- Sprints 3–4: Ramp Security & Compliance (APS-2); maintain Checkout velocity; scope freeze on APS-1 after Sprint 2 (per Decision D3).
- Sprints 5+: Integration and hardening across both workstreams, UAT, controlled go-live through M3.

## Success Criteria

- All four milestones (M0–M3) released on or before their target dates.
- Zero open High/Highest severity bugs at go-live.
- Payment-security compliance audit passed before M2 release date.
- Predictability ≥ 85% across last 3 sprints before launch.
- No unresolved cross-team blocking dependencies at M3 release.
